🎯 Niche Selection Checklist

The right niche makes everything easier. Use this checklist to validate yours.

Your Niche Must Pass All 5:

  • 💬 Conversation: People are already talking about it on X daily
  • 💰 Monetizable: There are products, courses, or services to sell/affiliate
  • 🎯 Your expertise: You have genuine knowledge or experience to share
  • 📈 Growing: The topic is trending up, not dying
  • 🔁 Evergreen: It'll still be relevant in 2 years
Pro tip: The best niches are at the intersection of what you know, what people ask about, and what has money flowing. Find that intersection.
